I've been writing software since 2016 and, for most of that time, the only public record of it was a GitHub profile and a CV that went out of date the week after I wrote it. This site is the fix.

It is a home for three things.

**The work.** Everything I build in the open now lives at [Prabhava Labs](https://prabhavalabs.com), and the [work page](/work) collects it alongside the things I built before that — ERPs, logistics systems, IoT irrigation, payment plugins.

**The thinking.** At Prabhava Labs every project ships with its code, its story, and the decisions behind it. This blog is the personal version of that: engineering write-ups when a problem deserves a long answer, trade-offs I got wrong, and notes on building software in and for Sri Lanka.

**The rest.** Now and then, something that has nothing to do with code.

If something here is useful, or wrong, [write to me](mailto:theekshana2@gmail.com). I read everything.
