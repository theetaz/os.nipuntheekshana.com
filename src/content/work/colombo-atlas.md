## Why this exists

Most of what I build answers a question. This one does not. I wanted a small piece of Colombo I could open in a browser tab and spend a few minutes in: the lake, the tower, the old station. Something closer to a postcard than a map.

## What you can do in it

**Explore.** Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and jump between aerial, city and lakeside views. A compass tells you which way you are facing.

**Visit the landmarks.** Lotus Tower, Altair, Gangaramaya Temple, the World Trade Center, Colombo Fort Station and the National Museum each have their own camera position and a short story about their history and architecture. Names appear in Sinhala, Tamil and English with their coordinates.

**Set the mood.** Morning light, soft daylight or golden hour; clear, cloudy, rain, heavy rain or a thunderstorm. Sky, shadows, water and the wetness of the ground all change together, which is the detail that makes it feel like weather rather than a filter.

**Put on the radio.** Live Sri Lankan stations play while you look around. A small badge shows the real temperature in Colombo right now, separate from whatever weather you have chosen for the scene.

## The models

The Station and the Museum have interiors and a cutaway view, so you can step into the ticket hall or look up the museum staircase. Every landmark can be opened in a model viewer, and most can be downloaded as GLB or as an editable Blender file.

Lotus Tower ships inside the compressed city model; the other five come with separate Blender sources and model packs. I also wrote up the reference-image approach behind them, so anyone can make their own landmark the same way.
