## The idea

I wanted a game that could only be set here: real Colombo streets, real coordinates, and a courier working their way up. The plan is a progression. You start on a bicycle, move to an electric bicycle and a motorbike, and eventually a car.

## What is playable today

The current build gives you a bicycle you can actually ride, three generated practice jobs, earnings that are saved on your device, and a north-up minimap. It all happens in a warm, illustrated 900 × 900 metre slice of the city around Lotus Tower.

## The part nobody sees: a study in movement

Most of the recent work is not in the game at all. It is a separate study covering a 1.8 × 1.8 kilometre district, with editable Blender scenes and a cyclist studio for getting movement right: walking, pedalling, and getting on and off the bicycle.

I review these the way I would review code, and I keep the record. The walking cycle has been approved. The pedalling track was approved earlier and is kept unchanged. The mounting and dismounting animations are ready for testing and **not** approved yet. Several earlier attempts at fitting the rider to the bicycle were rejected outright, and those are documented too.

## Being straight about what it is not

These are bounded prototypes. The delivery markers and the way roads are presented have not passed the field checks I would need before claiming they represent safe or legal real-world routes, so I do not claim that.

I would rather a small thing be true than a big thing be impressive.

## Where it stands

Incubating. It shares its city with [Colombo Atlas](/work/colombo-atlas), which is the calm version of the same streets.
